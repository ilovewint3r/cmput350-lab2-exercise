Answer written questions 1 and 2 here.
1) The coordinate systems are normal 2d, except the y-coordiate increases as it goes down rather than the typical convention of it going up. Typical angle calculation with the convention that y-coordinate increases as it goes up is that the orientation is counter-clockwise, which is where the formula (cos(theta), sin(theta)) makes sense. But because of how the y-coordinate increases as it goes down, the orientation is reversed so the angle calculation becomes (sin(theta), cos(theta)).
2) Having organized sections of code for specific purposes provides a level of encapsulation and abstraction from the details of how everything is coming (ex. filtering specific inputs doesn't need to be tied in with game logic, the game only needs to know that an input of a desired kind was made).
